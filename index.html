With over a decade of professional practice since 2013, my core strength lies in bridging digital technology, creative design, and print production. I develop web-based systems and databases, create visual communication materials, and prepare print-ready assets from concept and prepress through final production.

Currently serving at Adela Serra Ty Memorial Medical Center as a Graphic Designer & Computer Maintenance Technologist, my present role focuses on developing and supporting internal systems for my assigned office, creating institutional Information, Education, and Communication (IEC) materials, and providing technical support for office technology requirements. Looking ahead, I aim to contribute to the future development and deployment of more integrated digital systems that can support broader institutional operations and improve workflow efficiency.

A graduate of Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Surigao del Sur State University (Batch 2016) and currently pursuing graduate studies in Master of Science in Computer Science at North Eastern Mindanao State University — Tandag Campus, I combine technical discipline, creative problem-solving, and practical production experience in every system, design, and digital solution I build.
