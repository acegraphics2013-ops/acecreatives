PNA Caraga Nurses Convention 2026

A complete event visual system covering identity design, stage backdrops, social media assets, sublimated delegate apparel, and print collateral.

Role

Creative Director & Print Specialist

Deliverables
  • Official Convention Logo
  • 20ft Stage Backdrop Tarp
  • Full Sublimation Shirts
  • ID Badges & Certificates
  • Social Media Graphics

Project Overview

The Philippine Nurses Association (PNA) Caraga Regional Convention required a bold visual identity that represents nursing excellence and modern healthcare leadership in Caraga region. The branding was executed across both digital promotions and large-scale physical event production.

Execution

Designed in CorelDRAW and Photoshop, all print assets were calibrated to exact CMYK color profiles for large format tarpaulin printing and high-temperature apparel sublimation, ensuring exact color matching across all event touchpoints.
